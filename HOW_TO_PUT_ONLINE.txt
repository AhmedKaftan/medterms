HOW TO PUT THE APP ONLINE FOR iPAD AND ANDROID TABLETS
======================================================

Why: tablets cannot run the app properly from a zip file. Put it online once,
send students the link, and each student installs it and saves it for offline use.

A) Put it online (one time, about 5 minutes, on a computer)
  1. Unzip Medical_Terminology_Lectures_App_v1.0.zip on a computer.
  2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in Chrome or Edge and sign up free
     (with Google or e-mail) so the link stays permanently.
  3. Drag the whole folder "Medical_Terminology_Lectures_App" onto the page.
     Wait until the upload finishes (about 7,300 files, 170 MB).
  4. Netlify gives you a link such as https://something.netlify.app
     Under "Site configuration > Change site name" you can make it nicer,
     e.g. https://kufa-medterms.netlify.app
  5. Send that link to the students (WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Classroom).
  To update later: in Netlify open the site > Deploys and drag the new folder
  in again. Students get the new version automatically next time they open it.
  (Any other web host also works, for example the university web server:
   copy the folder as it is.)

B) What each student does (once, on Wi-Fi)
  iPad / iPhone (Safari):
    1. Open the link in Safari.
    2. Tap Share (square with arrow) > Add to Home Screen > Add.
    3. Open the app from the new "Med Terms" icon.
    4. On the home page tap "Tablet & offline" > "Save for offline" and wait
       until it says "All saved". Then it works with no internet.
  Android tablet / phone (Chrome):
    1. Open the link in Chrome.
    2. Tap "Tablet & offline" > "Install app" (or Chrome menu > Install app /
       Add to Home screen).
    3. Open the app from the new icon, tap "Tablet & offline" >
       "Save for offline" and wait until it says "All saved".

Notes
  - Progress (points, saved words, quiz results) is kept on each device.
  - Saving everything needs about 170 MB of free space on the tablet.
  - On a laptop or desktop the zip still works directly: open index.html.

طريقة نشر التطبيق للآيباد وأجهزة أندرويد
  أ) مرة واحدة على الحاسوب: فك ضغط الملف، ثم افتح https://app.netlify.com/drop
     وسجّل مجاناً، واسحب مجلد "Medical_Terminology_Lectures_App" كاملاً إلى الصفحة.
     ستحصل على رابط (مثل https://kufa-medterms.netlify.app) أرسله للطلبة.
  ب) الطالب (مرة واحدة على Wi-Fi):
     آيباد: افتح الرابط في Safari ← زر المشاركة ← "إضافة إلى الشاشة الرئيسية"،
     ثم افتح التطبيق من الأيقونة واضغط "Tablet & offline" ← "Save for offline".
     أندرويد: افتح الرابط في Chrome ← "Install app" أو القائمة ⋮ ← "تثبيت التطبيق"،
     ثم "Save for offline". بعدها يعمل التطبيق بدون إنترنت.
